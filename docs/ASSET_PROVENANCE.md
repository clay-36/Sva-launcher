# Visual asset provenance

On 2026-10-16 the maintainer confirmed ownership or redistribution permission for
the bundled logo, wallpapers and documentation screenshot content. This is a
maintainer attestation, not an independent verification of each image's origin.

This confirmation covers the existing project visual assets, including:

- `app/src/main/res/drawable/sva_image.png` and legacy Duo branding images.
- `app/src/main/res/drawable/default_wallpaper.jpg` and `mountain_wallpaper.png`.
- Images in `screenshots/` used to illustrate the app.

Manrope has its own bundled OFL license. Screensaver paper textures, geometric
planes, gradients and light effects are drawn by the app's source code.

Third-party marks, broadcasts, videos, games and artwork visible inside screenshots
retain their owners' rights; the app's GPL license does not relicense that content.
Future contributed images must include their source, author, license or explicit
redistribution permission. Use demonstration data and avoid credentials or private
family photographs in public screenshots.
