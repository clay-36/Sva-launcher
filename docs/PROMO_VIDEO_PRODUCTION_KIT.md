# Sva Launcher — Promo Video Production Kit

This kit contains the complete, production-ready blueprint for creating a 40–45 second promotional video for **Sva Launcher**, highlighting its core value proposition: a calm, remote-first Android TV home screen free from bloat and ads, featuring dynamic photo wallpapers, ambient **Sva Memories** screensavers, integrated Live TV, and built-in retro gaming.

---

## 1. Video Specifications & Compliance

| Parameter | Recommended Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | 42 – 45 seconds |
| **Resolution & FPS** | 1920×1080 (1080p) or 3840×2160 (4K) at 60 fps |
| **Aspect Ratio** | 16:9 Landscape (standard YouTube & Google Play Store) *(Optional: 9:16 vertical crop for Shorts/Reels)* |
| **Target Audio LUFS** | -14 LUFS (streaming standard for YouTube) |
| **Play Store Rule** | YouTube video link must **not** have monetization enabled, and must not contain mid-roll or pre-roll ads. |

### Scene 1: The Hook (0:00 – 0:05)
* **Visual**: Brief 1-second glimpse of a chaotic, ad-saturated default TV launcher. A smooth diagonal wipe or push transition cuts into **Sva Launcher's clean Home screen** with a stunning high-res landscape backdrop.

## 6. How to Capture and Assemble the Video

### Step 1: Capture Raw Clips with the Automated Script
Connect your Android TV device or emulator via ADB, then run:

```bash
./scripts/record-promo.sh
```

Follow the prompts to record:
1. `scene1_home_wallpapers` (10s)
2. `scene2_screensaver_memories` (15s)
3. `scene3_livetv_guide` (12s)
4. `scene4_retro_games` (10s)
5. `scene5_calendar_weather` (10s)

The script automatically pulls the clips and converts them to clean 60fps MP4s inside `promo/recordings/`.

### Step 2: Assemble in Your Video Editor (DaVinci Resolve / CapCut / Premiere)
1. **Frame & Mockup**: Place clips inside a sleek 16:9 minimalist modern TV bezel frame (PNG mockup) over a gently blurred, warm interior background.
2. **Motion**: Apply subtle 105% scale punch-ins on focus transitions to keep the viewer engaged.
3. **Audio Balancing**:
   - Background Music: -20 dB to -24 dB under voiceover; swell to -14 dB during outro.
   - Voiceover: Centered, compressed, EQ'd with warm bass and clear presence.
   - Remote Clicks & SFX: -16 dB.
4. **Export**: H.264 / H.265 at 1920×1080 or 3840×2160, 60fps, Constant Bitrate (CBR 25-40 Mbps for 1080p, 50-80 Mbps for 4K).
